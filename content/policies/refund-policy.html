Changing your mind about an online order (14 days)

If you buy online, you can cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of the day you receive your item (or collect it). To cancel, tell us clearly by email or phone – you can use the model cancellation wording below, but you don't have to.

You must then return the item within 14 days of telling us. The 14-day cancellation right does not apply to: items bought in our shop; personalised items; sealed hygiene items (for example earrings, cosmetics) once unsealed; and sealed audio, video or software once unsealed.

Faulty items – your statutory rights

Every item you buy from us – including clearance, open-box, customer-return, used and unchecked items – must be as described, of satisfactory quality (taking its stated condition and price into account) and fit for purpose. If it isn't:

  • within 30 days you can reject it for a full refund;
  • after 30 days and within 6 months, you're entitled to a repair or replacement, or a refund if that isn't possible;
  • after 6 months you may still have rights, but you may need to show the fault was there when you bought it.

Faults that we told you about before you bought (for example in the product's condition notes) are not grounds for a return as faulty. Our condition labels never reduce your statutory rights.
