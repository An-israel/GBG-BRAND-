# P2 – post-launch estimates (not built now)

Rough estimates for planning; each needs a short scoping chat and owner approval. Costs exclude any app subscription.

| Feature | What it means | Approach | Effort | Running cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Automated product drops | Scheduled "drop" of new stock at a set time with a teaser | Shopify scheduled publishing + a "Coming soon" section and New Arrivals announcement | 2–3 days | Free (built-in) |
| Reviews | Customer product/shop reviews | Shopify Product Reviews replacement apps (e.g. Judge.me free tier) – for one-off items, shop-level reviews are more useful | 1–2 days | £0–£15/month |
| Advanced email flows | Welcome series, browse/basket abandonment, win-back | Shopify Email + Shopify Flow/Marketing automations, branded templates | 3–4 days | Shopify Email free allowance, then per-email |
| Loyalty | Points or stamps for repeat shoppers, online + in shop | App (e.g. Smile.io / Shopify-native loyalty partners) tied to POS | 2–3 days | £0–£50/month |
| Expanded catalogue | From 100 to 300+ listings | Same CSV pipeline; mainly staff time; add Fashion size filters | Staff time + 1 day dev | – |
| Advanced merchandising | Bundles, "complete the look", seasonal landing pages | Shopify Bundles app, new section types | 3–5 days | Free–low |
| Ad integrations | Google Shopping, Meta and TikTok catalogues + conversion tracking | Official Shopify channels, consent-aware pixels, feed hygiene (titles, GTIN/condition) | 2–3 days | Free apps; ad spend separate |
