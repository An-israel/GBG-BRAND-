# GBG Bargain Shop – site map (plain English)

This is how the website is organised. The menu builds itself: a category only appears once it has
products, and a help page only appears once it exists. Nothing in the menu can lead to an empty page.

```
GBG Bargain Shop  (home page)
│
├── Shop ▾                         every category, with its sub-categories
│   ├── Bags & Accessories         Handbags · Backpacks · Purses · Travel
│   ├── Shoes                      Women · Men · Kids
│   ├── Jewellery & Watches        Earrings · Necklaces · Bracelets · Watches
│   ├── Fashion                    Women · Men · Kids
│   ├── Home & Kitchen             Cookware · Storage · Household essentials
│   ├── Electronics                Speakers · Small electronics · Accessories
│   ├── Beauty                     (only if there is stock)
│   └── All products
│
├── New in                         the newest stock, newest first
│
├── Deals ▾  (yellow)              Under £5 · Clearance · Last chance · Best bargains
│
├── Visit us                       address, opening hours, directions, map, Click & Collect
│
└── Help ▾                         Contact us · FAQs · Click & Collect · Delivery ·
                                   Returns & refunds · About us · Site map · Call 07344 670317
```

## Where people find it

| Place | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Computer – top menu** | Shop · New in · Deals · Visit us · Help. "Shop" opens a big panel with every category. |
| **Phone – "MENU" button** (top left) | A search box, three big buttons (New in · Deals · Visit us), then "Shop by category" (tap a category to see its sub-categories), then Help, then phone/WhatsApp. |
| **Phone – quick links** (under the search bar) | One-tap chips: New in · Deals · Visit us · each category. Swipe sideways for more. |
| **Footer** (bottom of every page) | Shop (all categories and deals) · Help & information · Opening hours · contact details. |
| **Site map page** (`/collections`, linked in Help and the footer) | Everything above on one page. |

## Before the shop is fully set up

Straight after the theme is installed (before the GBG collections and pages are created), the menu
uses the shop's **real existing collections** instead and shows **Shop · Visit us · Help**.
"Visit us" scrolls to the Visit us block on the home page until the Visit Our Shop page is published.
**New in** and **Deals** appear by themselves as soon as those collections have products.

## Changing the menu

Nothing needs to be done – it updates itself. If the owner ever wants a hand-made menu instead:
Online Store → Themes → Customize → Header → **Menu: "A Shopify menu"**, then choose the menu
(edited in Online Store → Navigation).
